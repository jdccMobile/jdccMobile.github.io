Hero Realms health: starting totals and how to track them

In a standard Hero Realms duel both players start at 50 health. Character Packs change that — each class has its own starting health — and campaign bosses go far beyond it.

Here is what the totals look like across modes, and the cleanest way to keep them straight.

Standard duels: 50 each

The base game mirrors Star Realms: 50 health, combat lowers it, gold-and-ally engines heal it. First player to zero loses. Two or three health swings per turn are normal once engines come online.

Character Packs change the math

Playing with Character Packs, each class starts at its own total (roughly 50–55 depending on the class) and adds a one-use skill that often heals or deals damage. That means asymmetric starting totals — exactly the thing flip cards handle worst.

Campaigns and bosses

In The Ruin of Thandar and other campaign content, heroes level up and bosses run totals well past 50. You need a tracker that accepts any starting number, not one hard-coded to 50.

One habit worth keeping

Announce damage and healing out loud as you tap (“6 to you, +3 me”). The counter keeps the number; the announcement keeps the trust.
