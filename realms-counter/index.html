Frequently asked questions

How do you track authority in Star Realms?

Each player starts at 50 authority; combat lowers it and some cards restore it. The app replaces the paper authority cards with a fast tap counter. Learn more

What is the starting health in Hero Realms?

Both players start at 50 in standard games; campaign characters can differ. The app lets you set any starting value. Learn more

Can I use it with 3 or 4 players?

Yes, up to 4 players on one device — ideal for free-for-all or Hunter formats. Learn more

Does it work for Magic: The Gathering and other games?

Yes. It works as a life counter for any game: MTG, Cthulhu Realms, Ascension and more. Learn more

Does the app track wins?

Yes. It keeps each player's win ratio, so you finally know who the real champion of your group is.
