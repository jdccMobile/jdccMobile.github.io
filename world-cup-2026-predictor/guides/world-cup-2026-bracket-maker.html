How to make a World Cup 2026 bracket, step by step

To make a World Cup 2026 bracket you need three things: predictions for the 12 group tables, the 8 best third-placed teams, and knockout picks from the Round of 32 to the final. The tricky part is step two — the thirds follow an official FIFA placement table.

You can do it on paper, in a spreadsheet, or let an app handle the rules for you. Here is the full process either way.

Step 1: predict all 12 groups

Order the four teams of every group from first to fourth. If you want more precision, predict each of the 72 group matches — points, goal difference and goals scored decide the table, and those tiebreakers matter for which thirds advance.

Step 2: rank the third-placed teams

Take the 12 third-placed teams and rank them by points, then goal difference, then goals scored. The top 8 advance. On paper this is where most brackets go wrong: which third lands in which Round of 32 slot depends on FIFA's combination table, not on your choice.

Step 3: fill the knockout rounds

With the Round of 32 set, pick a winner for every tie: 32 → 16 → quarter-finals → semi-finals → final. Eight rounds of picks and you have your champion. Be bold in one or two spots — every World Cup kills at least one favourite early.
