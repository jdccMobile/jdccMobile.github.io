Frequently asked questions

What is Kawashi, the kawaii sushi counter?

Kawashi is a free app that counts the sushi pieces you eat. Every piece you log makes your on-screen kawaii sushi grow, and the app saves your best score automatically.

How much sushi can the average person eat?

Most people eat between 8 and 12 pieces in one sitting, and big eaters at buffets often reach 20 or more. With a counter you know your exact number. Learn more

Does the app save my sushi record?

Yes. Kawashi automatically saves your best score, so next time you visit a Japanese restaurant you can try to beat your own high score.

How do I win a sushi eating challenge with friends?

Set clear rules — same piece type, same time limit — and count every piece honestly in the app. Highest verified count wins. Learn more

How many calories are in a piece of sushi?

A typical nigiri has 40–70 kcal and a maki piece 20–45 kcal. Knowing your piece count lets you estimate the whole meal. Learn more

Is Kawashi free and available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Kawashi is free to download on both the App Store and Google Play.
