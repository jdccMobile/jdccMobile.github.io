Frequently asked questions

How do you keep score in Chinchón?

After each round, every player adds the value of their unmelded cards. Closing with everything melded subtracts 10 points; a chinchón wins outright. The app does the math for you. Learn more

How do you play Chinchón?

Each player gets 7 cards and tries to form runs or sets, drawing and discarding each turn. Stay under the agreed limit to win. Learn more

What is the difference between Chinchón, Conga and Golpe?

They are regional variants of the same rummy-style game with small differences in closing and scoring. The app supports all three. Learn more

Can players re-enter after being eliminated?

Yes. The app supports re-entries: an eliminated player comes back with the highest active score, just like at a real table.

Does the Chinchón counter work offline?

Yes, completely. Record rounds, save games and resume them later without any connection.

Is the Chinchón score counter free?

Yes. The app is free to download on the App Store and Google Play.
